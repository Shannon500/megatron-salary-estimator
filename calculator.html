Salary & Career Information

Understanding your salary can help you plan your spending, savings and career goals.

Hourly Pay

Hourly pay is the amount you earn for each hour you work. Your weekly income can be estimated by multiplying your hourly rate by the number of hours worked.

Overtime Pay

Overtime may be paid at a higher rate than regular hours. Megatron allows you to enter your overtime hours and estimated overtime multiplier.

Take-Home Pay

Take-home pay is the amount remaining after estimated deductions. Use the deduction field to create a simple estimate of your net earnings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Megatron Salary Estimator free?

Yes. The calculator is designed to be free to use.

Can I calculate overtime?

Yes. Enter your overtime hours and choose an overtime rate to include overtime in your estimated earnings.

Can I calculate my take-home pay?

Yes. Enter an estimated deduction percentage and Megatron will calculate an estimated take-home amount.

Are the tax calculations official?

No. The deduction feature is only an estimate and should not be treated as official tax advice.
