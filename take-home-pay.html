Take-Home Pay Calculator

Your take-home pay is the amount you have left after deductions are taken from your gross income.

Megatron allows you to estimate your take-home pay using an estimated deduction percentage.

Example

If your weekly gross income is KSh 47,500 and your estimated deductions are 10%:

Estimated deductions: KSh 4,750

Estimated take-home pay: KSh 42,750
